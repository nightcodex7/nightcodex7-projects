Privacy Policy

nightcode.co.in apps · Last updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to nightcode.co.in and the Android applications published by nightcodex7, including Yet Another LuCI App for OpenWrt. It explains how data is collected, processed, and safeguarded across both our open-source Community Edition and official distribution builds. The App operates on a local-first architecture, is 100% Ad-Free, and does not collect or process Advertising IDs or personal user data.

1. Information We Collect & Process

a. Local Router Credentials & Local Network Data

  • Apps such as Yet Another LuCI App require your router's IP address, port, username, and password to establish direct HTTP/HTTPS and JSON-RPC connections with your device.
  • These credentials are encrypted and stored locally on your device using hardware-backed secure storage (via flutter_secure_storage / KeyStore).
  • We do not collect, transmit, upload, or store your router credentials, IP addresses, network layout, or router configurations on any external server or developer database.

b. Local Network Access Permission

  • To discover, monitor, and manage OpenWrt routers on modern Android versions (Android 15+ / API 35+ / API 37), the App requests the ACCESS_LOCAL_NETWORK runtime permission.
  • This permission is used strictly to establish socket connections to local gateway IP addresses (such as 192.168.1.1 or 10.0.0.1).

2. App Distribution Flavors & Third-Party Services

a. Community Edition (GitHub Releases / FOSS)

  • 100% Free & Ad-Free: Contains zero advertisements, does not collect Advertising IDs (AD_ID), and does not integrate third-party ad networks.
  • No In-App Purchases: Fully open-source under the Apache License 2.0 with unlimited router profiles.
  • Zero Telemetry or Analytics: Contains no background tracking, crash telemetry, or third-party analytics SDKs.

b. Official Release / Play Store Edition

  • Support the Developer & In-App Billing (Google Play Billing): The App may offer optional voluntary "Support the Developer" contributions, tip jars, or feature tier upgrades. All transactions are processed securely by the Google Play Store. The App does not collect or store credit card details or billing addresses.
  • 100% Ad-Free Application: The App contains zero advertisements across all distribution builds, does not collect or process Advertising IDs (AD_ID), and does not serve ads or track users via third-party advertising networks.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Local Router Management: Direct communication with your OpenWrt router to display status, interfaces, firewall rules, and connected clients.
  • Voluntary Contributions & Entitlements: Verifying active purchase receipts via Google Play Services to unlock developer support tiers.
  • Ad-Free Experience: No advertising data, device identifiers, or tracking metrics are ever collected or processed for ad serving.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

  • Router Data: Zero router credentials or router configuration data are ever shared with third parties.
  • Billing & Services: Non-sensitive purchase receipts for voluntary developer support are processed exclusively through Google Play Services in compliance with Google's Privacy Policy. No advertising IDs or user tracking IDs are shared or processed.

5. Security

  • Router credentials and session tokens remain stored securely on your local device.
  • The App supports HTTPS connections, custom ports, and SSL verification options for secure local network and VPN traffic.

6. Children's Privacy (Age 3+ Compliance)

  • Rated Age 3+ (Suitable for All Ages): Yet Another LuCI App is listed and rated Age 3+ on the Google Play Store, making it suitable for users of all ages, including children under 13.
  • Zero Data Collection from Children: The App operates on a local-first architecture and does not collect, store, or share any personal information, personal identifiers, device IDs, location data, or network layout details from children or any other users.
  • Ad-Free & No Tracking: The App displays zero advertisements and includes no third-party analytics or user tracking, ensuring a completely safe environment for family and child use.
Play Console Data Safety Notice: The Google Play Console “Data Safety” declarations accurately reflect that the App operates local-first for all network management tasks, is 100% Ad-Free (no Advertising IDs collected), and discloses optional Financial / Purchase History (Google Play Billing) where voluntary support features are accessed in Play Store distribution builds. This privacy policy page is publicly accessible, active, and available at a permanent HTTPS URL.
