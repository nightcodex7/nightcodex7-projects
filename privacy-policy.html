Privacy Policy

nightcode.co.in apps · Last updated: August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to nightcode.co.in and the Android applications published by nightcodex7, including Yet Another LuCI App for OpenWrt. It explains how data is collected, used, and safeguarded in the apps and on this website, including third-party SDKs such as Google AdMob and Google Play Billing.

1. Information We Collect

a. User Credentials & Local Router Data

  • Apps such as Yet Another LuCI App require your router's IP address, username, and password to connect to your device.
  • These credentials are stored securely on your device using industry-standard hardware-backed encryption (for example, via flutter_secure_storage).
  • We do not collect, transmit, or store your router credentials or router configuration data on external servers.

b. Third-Party Ad Services (Google AdMob & UMP)

  • For free-tier users, the apps may display advertisements powered by Google AdMob.
  • Data Collected: Google AdMob may automatically collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), IP address, coarse location, app usage, and interaction data for ad serving, personalization, and analytics.
  • GDPR & UK User Consent: When required by regional privacy laws, the apps use the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to request user consent before ads are personalized or served. Users can manage or revoke consent preferences where applicable.
  • Some future apps may be ad-free. This policy is written to remain flexible for both ad-supported and ads-free releases.

c. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (Google Play Billing)

  • The apps may offer optional paid upgrades or subscriptions to remove ads, unlock premium features, or expand router support.
  • All payment transactions and billing are handled by the Google Play Store and associated Google Play Billing services.
  • Data Handled: The apps may receive purchase receipts, subscription entitlement state, and purchase validation details from Google Play. The apps do not collect or store payment card numbers, bank account information, or billing addresses.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Router Management: Authenticating directly with your router to retrieve and display status, interfaces, firewall rules, and connected clients.
  • Advertising: Displaying relevant banner ads to free-tier users via AdMob.
  • Entitlement Enforcement: Verifying active subscription or lifetime purchases to remove ads and unlock multi-router features.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

  • Router Data: Zero router credentials or router configuration data are shared with third parties.
  • Ad & Billing Partners: Advertising IDs, purchase receipts, and related validation data may be shared with Google AdMob and Google Play Services in accordance with Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Security

  • Router credentials remain stored locally on your device.
  • The App supports HTTPS connections and custom port configurations for secure communication with your router.

5. Children's Privacy

  • The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
Play Console Data Safety Notice: The Google Play Console “Data Safety” declarations should match this policy and accurately disclose that the apps may collect Device or other IDs (such as Advertising ID) through Google AdMob and Financial Info / Purchase History through Google Play Billing. This privacy policy page is publicly accessible, active, and not a PDF.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out:
